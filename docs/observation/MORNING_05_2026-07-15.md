## Root-cause hypotheses to trace next

These are hypotheses, not build decisions.

1. **Brief follow-up grounding is reading broad dashboard state, not the Cap 50 synthesized cluster object.**
   Evidence: `What matters most from this brief?` and `Is this brief complete or still partial?` dumped weather, calendar, individual headlines, and `last_external_call`, but not the brief's `placeholder`/`synthesis_status` or synthesized cluster summaries.

2. **Category selection uses broad `global` news bucket, not the more specific `Global Security` brief cluster.**
   Evidence: `summarize the global security stories` rendered `CATEGORY SUMMARY - Global News` and included a clean-energy story.

3. **Structured brief commands are under-routed for natural phrasing.**
   Evidence: `track story N` worked, but `compare story 1 and story 2` fell to advisory chat instead of Cap 50 `compare_clusters`.
   Code trace sharpened after logging: the existing compare regex accepts `compare story 1 and 2`,
   but not the repeated-word phrasing `compare story 1 and story 2`.

4. **The synthesis-ready event may not be human-legible in the chat transcript when it arrives during or near manual interaction.**
   Evidence: transcript shows `Synthesis ready` followed by an unrelated advisory greeting, not a clear refreshed brief. This needs a controlled reproduction before assuming a frontend bug.

5. **News-category phrasings can still fall through to advisory chat.**
   Evidence: `show me global security news` produced a fabricated placeholder aggregator link from local advisory chat. This should be treated as a routing/truth-integrity trace target because it presents unsourced inference in a news shape.

6. **Numeric story commands lack an active-surface contract.**
   Evidence: after a category summary became the visible/current news surface, `track story 2` still resolved against the older intelligence brief and returned `Technology`. Story indices need a surface/version identity, not just the last broad story list.

7. **Confidence display has two sources of truth.**
   Evidence: the brief body showed `Confidence: Medium-Low`, while the surrounding message trust strip showed `Confidence: Standard`. This should be tracked as a presentation/trust defect, not as a constrained-generation issue.
