# docs/archive — Historical material

> **ARCHIVED — not runtime authority.** Nothing in this folder describes what Nova does today.
> Do not use anything here to make implementation, capability, or governance decisions.

For current truth, start at [`../CANONICAL/00_INDEX.md`](../CANONICAL/00_INDEX.md). The
authoritative live status is the generated
[`../current_runtime/CURRENT_RUNTIME_STATE.md`](../current_runtime/CURRENT_RUNTIME_STATE.md).

## What is in here

- **Superseded truth documents** — older "Nova Truth" / "Canonical Truth" versions and the
  constitutional blueprints, kept for lineage.
- **Closed-phase material** — `phase 1/`, `phase 2/`, `phase 3/`, `phase 3.5/`, `phase 4/`
  working docs, status, and design from phases that are complete.
- **`audits-2026-04/`** — the April 2026 audit strays (`AUDIT_ACTION_PLAN`, `SANITY_AUDIT`,
  `SECOND_PASS_OVERVIEW`) moved out of the `docs/` root on 2026-07-08.
- **Orphaned capability specs** — draft `.txt` specifications for capabilities that have since
  shipped (Governed Web Intelligence for Cap 16 + 48; Governed Webpage Launch for Cap 17).
- **Stray assets** — a few misfiled `.css`/`.py` files left here historically; they are not
  part of the running system.

## Why keep it

History is evidence of how Nova got here and what was already considered. The policy is to
**move and label, not delete** — see
[`../CANONICAL/08_ARCHIVE_POLICY.md`](../CANONICAL/08_ARCHIVE_POLICY.md). Deletion is reserved
for provable, safe duplicates.

## Other archive locations (not folded in)

Some historical material is intentionally kept nearer its subject rather than moved here:

- `docs/design/archive/` and `docs/design/archive(phase 4)/` — design-local archives, retained
  in place by the Phase 6 archive audit. Both carry their own archived banners.

Whether to fold those design archives into this folder is an open, low-priority question tracked
in `docs/todo/DOC_CLEANUP.md`; it is not done because it would rot ~14 cross-references and
overrides a prior ratified decision without a human call.

## What is deliberately NOT here

Proof packets, demo captures, and live-verification records are **not** archived even when old —
they are dated evidence and stay under `docs/PROOFS/`, `docs/demo_proof/`, and
`docs/capability_verification/`. See
[`../CANONICAL/06_TEST_AND_PROOF_TRUTH.md`](../CANONICAL/06_TEST_AND_PROOF_TRUTH.md).
